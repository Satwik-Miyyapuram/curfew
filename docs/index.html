Curfew

A distraction blocker for your phone and your PC that keeps them in step with no server, no account, and no subscription — and that can take its orders from your calendar.

Download Source on GitHub

What it does

Blocks on both devices at once

Start a session on your phone and your PC is blocked too. The devices talk directly, over a folder you already sync or a code you scan across a table.

Reads your calendar

A rule can say “whenever an event's title contains focus, run the deep-work profile.” Book the time once and the blocking follows it.

Locks you meant to set

A timer, your device PIN, a typing challenge, a restart, or another device's say-so. Every one of them shows what it is waiting for.

Costs nothing, ever

No subscription and no paid tier, because there is no server to pay for. AGPL-3.0, so it stays that way.

Getting started

Grab a build from Releases. Windows ships as a zip with a curfew.exe that is both the service and the command line; Android ships as an unsigned APK, which you can also build yourself.

Then, on either device, in the same three steps:

curfew add-profile curfew.toml --id deep-work
curfew block      curfew.toml --profile deep-work --site reddit.com
curfew add-window curfew.toml --id mornings --profile deep-work --from 09:00 --to 12:00

On the phone the same three steps are the Schedule and Apps tabs, and either device can be the one you set it up on.

What it will not do

Curfew is for restricting your own devices. There is no parent role, no admin role, no remote monitoring of somebody else's phone, and no hidden mode. Every lock is visible from the moment it starts and every lock offers a 24-hour delayed release, so a block cannot be made invisible or permanent — including by somebody who installed it on a device that is not theirs. If that is what you are looking for, this is the wrong project.

It also will not pretend to be unbeatable. Somebody with root, an unlocked bootloader, or an Administrator account they are willing to use can get past it, and so can a second device you never told it about. What it stops is the impulse, and the moment of weakness ten minutes later.
