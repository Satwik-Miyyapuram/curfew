Three things, then it works

Curfew blocks from a Windows service, so it keeps going when this window is closed, when you sign out, and after a restart.

If Windows said “Windows protected your PC”, that is SmartScreen reporting that nobody paid for a code-signing certificate — true of this app, and true of most free software. The way to check a build is its hash and its source, both published for every release. Neither the hash nor the source depends on trusting us.
Curfew cannot stop someone with administrator rights and an hour. It is a lock on a cupboard, not a safe — it is here to make a decision you already made stick.
