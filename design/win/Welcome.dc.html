Curfew✕
Curfew is running.
The icon by the clock is where blocks are ended. Two things worth knowing once.
⚠️
Windows may have called this app unrecognised.
Curfew is not signed with a code-signing certificate — those are paid every year and this project has no revenue. The service also closes other applications' windows, which is behaviour scanners watch for. The source is public and the builds are reproducible from it.
🛡️
This icon is not what does the blocking.
The service enforces with it hidden, closed or ended. Hiding the icon is not a way out of a session.
